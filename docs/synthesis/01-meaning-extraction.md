# S1 — Meaning Extraction

Status: PROVISIONAL / ACTIVE  
Date: 2026-10-07

## Why this step exists

The source atlas contains too many observations to design from directly.

Features, technologies and business models must not become principles simply because they are exciting.

The first synthesis reduces the research into seven meanings that currently survive across multiple domains.

They are provisional and may be merged, renamed or rejected after exploration.

# 1. RELATIONAL LIFE

## Meaning

The living thing cannot be understood separately from:

- place
- environmental conditions
- people
- purpose
- care system

The useful unit is not merely "plant."

It is closer to:

> living thing in relation to an environment

## Absorbs

- plant / site fit
- light and humidity context
- zone / spatial purpose
- human flow
- placement
- professional maintenance context

## Guardrail

Do not reduce the product to a flat inventory of plant objects.

# 2. LIVING TIME

## Meaning

A living installation is never fully complete at handover.

Growth, stress, intervention, recovery and change continue.

> Time is part of the product.

## Absorbs

- growth
- delayed feedback
- recurring maintenance
- season / rhythm
- before / after
- state change
- design survival over time

## Guardrail

Do not represent life as a static healthy / sick snapshot.

# 3. RETAINED HISTORY

## Meaning

What happened before affects how the present is understood.

The system should retain the meaningful chain:

> observation → decision → intervention → result

History may contain operational, biological, spatial and human meaning.

## Guardrail

Do not turn history into a noisy activity feed.

# 4. CARE CREATES RELATIONSHIP

## Meaning

Care is not only maintenance after the product.

In many contexts, care itself creates understanding, confidence and attachment.

## Core tension

> meaningful involvement ↔ burden

## Guardrail

Do not assume maximum automation is maximum value.

# 5. LIVING AGENCY

## Meaning

A plant is not passive material.

It senses, regulates, responds and changes.

The environment acts on life, and life acts back on the environment.

## Guardrail

Do not anthropomorphize biological response into unsupported claims about emotion or consciousness.

# 6. LEGIBILITY

## Meaning

Data is not the goal.

Technology may make a living state easier to understand without replacing the living thing with numbers.

> Technology should translate living state, not substitute for it.

## Guardrail

No dashboards, charts, notifications or AI explanations unless they reveal meaningful change.

# 7. ACCOUNTABLE LIVING

## Meaning

Living value should leave evidence.

Important claims should distinguish between:

- measured
- observed
- estimated
- modeled
- inferred
- unknown

This applies to both service quality and environmental / impact claims.

## Guardrail

No greenwashing and no fake precision.

## Current non-principles

These remain possible tools or expressions, not core principles:

- AI
- sensors
- IoT
- QR codes
- subscription
- client portal
- digital twin
- diagnosis
- green wall
- mobile app
- dashboard
- 3D
- computer vision
