# Plantupia — Working Law

Status: ACTIVE / PROVISIONAL  
Date: 2026-10-07

## 1. Why this repository exists

Plantupia is not being approached as a predefined app project.

The current task is to discover what product, service, physical system, software layer, or hybrid direction is actually worth building.

The repository therefore preserves the path from evidence to decision instead of documenting only the final answer.

## 2. Canonical discovery loop

Reality → Market → Source / domain research → Reference decomposition → Meaning extraction → Product translation → Exploration → Prototype evidence → Critique / QA → Research recheck → Product thesis → Production workflow

This is not a waterfall. Any later stage may reopen an earlier assumption when evidence changes.

## 3. Research law

Research is not limited to direct competitors.

Useful references may come from:

- horticulture and plant biology
- interiorscaping and facilities operations
- architecture and workplace design
- furniture and industrial design
- IoT and sensing
- digital twins and asset lifecycle systems
- interactive art and ambient interfaces
- behavioral research
- recurring service businesses
- consumer products
- professional software

A reference is valuable when it reveals a relationship, operating principle, interaction law, commercial mechanism, or failure mode.

Do not copy the visual answer of a reference.

## 4. Evidence law

Keep these categories separate:

- Public evidence: directly supported by a public source.
- Private context: known from company or personal communication; do not publish unless explicitly approved.
- Working hypothesis: plausible interpretation that still needs testing.
- Design principle: a synthesis from multiple observations.
- Product decision: only after evidence and exploration justify commitment.

Never silently promote a hypothesis into a fact.

## 5. Creative law

The workflow repeats. The answer must not.

The current principles are allowed to change. Their job is to make exploration more intelligent, not to constrain it prematurely.

Do not force Plantupia into the visual language or product architecture of Fokhara, HILTECH, Nova, ORTH, Naava, or any other reference.

## 6. Technology law

Technology is admitted only when it changes a real relationship, decision, capability, experience, or business outcome.

Do not add:

- AI because the category expects AI
- sensors because "green tech" sounds more credible with hardware
- dashboards because data exists
- automation because manual interaction looks inefficient
- WebGL or 3D because a creative product should look advanced

Concept chooses the tool.

## 7. Human / living-system law

A living thing is not only information to be read.

Care, observation, touch, time, uncertainty, and physical handling may be part of the value.

Working rule:

> Automate the burden, not the relationship.

## 8. Public repository safety

This repository is public.

Do not commit:

- offer-letter compensation or personal employment details
- private company messages
- confidential roadmap details not already public
- client-confidential materials
- credentials, tokens, internal URLs, private files, or personal data

Private context may guide internal thinking without being reproduced here.
