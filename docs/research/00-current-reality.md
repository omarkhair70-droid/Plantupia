# R0 — Current Reality

Status: ACTIVE / PUBLIC-SAFE SNAPSHOT  
Date: 2026-10-07

## What is known publicly

Plantupia operates in the commercial greenery / green-space domain rather than being only a consumer plant shop.

The public direction visible through company and founder material points toward:

- commercial indoor and outdoor greenery
- offices, hospitality, restaurants and similar business spaces
- design, plant selection, placement and implementation
- an ongoing relationship with the installed environment rather than a one-time decorative object

The earlier Plantophobia footprint is relevant as historical context because it shows movement from individual plant retail toward a broader B2B green-space proposition.

## What is intentionally not recorded here

Private company discussions and private documents have also informed the research process.

Because this repository is public, those materials are not reproduced. Any future use of private context must remain clearly separated from public evidence.

## Current discovery problem

The wrong framing is:

> What app should Plantupia build?

The current framing is:

> What relationship between people, plants, spaces, service, design, and technology can Plantupia uniquely own?

Possible outputs remain open:

- software
- web product
- mobile product
- professional design tool
- consumer subscription
- connected physical object
- smart furniture / living installation
- service operating system
- sensor layer
- recurring physical + digital service
- a combination of several surfaces around one core

None is locked.

## Current working advantage

Plantupia may have access to a combination that many pure-software competitors do not:

1. real physical installations
2. recurring exposure to living systems over time
3. spatial / aesthetic design
4. field operations and maintenance
5. opportunity to build digital products around real-world evidence

This is a hypothesis about strategic leverage, not yet a product thesis.

## Immediate research question

Before selecting a product category, determine:

- what changes when greenery becomes part of architecture rather than decoration
- what changes when a living installation must remain successful over time
- what knowledge is currently lost between design, installation, care and later decisions
- what parts of care are burden versus meaningful human involvement
- what the plant itself is already sensing and responding to
- what technology should reveal, automate, preserve or leave alone
